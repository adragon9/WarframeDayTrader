# Warframe Day Trader

## What does it do?

- Collects data from warframe.market and gives several price recommendations based off of orders from traders who are in game.
- Compares the highest buyer and lowest seller to tell you if it might be a good idea to just sell it now for a quick profit.

## How do I use it?

Just type in whatever, if it exists in warframe market you'll find it. If not it will let you know that it didn't find it.

## Disclaimer

All recommendations are based pretty much off my experiences so take that for what you will.
